# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS single page, no build step; hosted on GitHub Pages like the owner's previous site.

## Users

Homeowners and small businesses in Pedro Muñoz (Ciudad Real, 13620) and surroundings who need air conditioning or heating installed, serviced or repaired — often urgently in summer heat or winter cold — plus owners of Kaysun, Midea, Kosner, Coolwell or Vivax equipment looking for the brand's official technical service. Mostly on phones.

## Product Purpose

Website for Hartielectrocool (Harti Electrocool Climatización). Success = visitors request a quote (guided form that opens WhatsApp with the details pre-written) or call, and trust the company enough to do it.

## Positioning

Local climate specialist that is also the official technical service and authorised installer for Kaysun (by Frigicoll), Midea, Kosner, Coolwell and Vivax — sells its own equipment and repairs other brands.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: installation, maintenance, repair / technical service, heating / aerotermia.
- Own catalogue of air conditioners: content not defined yet; start by system types (split, conductos, cassette, multisplit, aerotermia) with quote on request; models added later.
- Hours: Monday–Friday 9:00–14:00 and 16:00–19:00; Saturday and Sunday closed.
- Quote flow: guided form → WhatsApp with prefilled message, plus direct call. No backend.
- Language: Spanish.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: HARTI ELECTROCOOL — CLIMATIZACIÓN.
- Logo (assets/logo-original.jpg): circle split into an orange-to-red warm half and a cyan-to-blue cool half with an orange-to-yellow lightning bolt in the centre; heavy rounded sans wordmark arched around it ("HARTI" orange, "ELECTROCOOL" blue, "CLIMATIZACIÓN" black).
- Official service / authorised installer banner supplied by the owner (assets/marcas-original.png).

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo, brand partnership banner, opening hours.
- Pending: phone/WhatsApp, street address, email, socials, service area, photos of installations, catalogue models and prices. Do not invent reviews, years of experience, installation counts, prices or certifications beyond those supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Asking for a quote takes under a minute and works from a phone.
2. Trust first: official brand service is visible early.
3. Heat and cold are the product: the page should make comfort tangible.
4. Real over invented: no fake reviews, stats or prices.
